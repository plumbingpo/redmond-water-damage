DIY vs Professional Water Damage Restoration in Redmond, WA

Home / Blog / DIY vs Professional Water Damage Restoration in Redmond, WA

After a leak or flood at a house in Redmond, WA, you have to decide quickly between doing the cleanup yourself and hiring professionals. The instinct to save money by doing it yourself is understandable, but most water damage is far more complex than it appears on the surface. Read on to learn where the line sits between a do-it-yourself job and one that calls for professional restoration.

When DIY Cleanup May Be Appropriate

You may not need a professional when the water event is tiny and well contained. Clean water, such as a sink overflow, that touched only non-porous hard floors, stayed below roughly 10 square feet, and was removed within the hour is usually fine to deal with yourself. Likewise, you most likely can skip the professional when a minor spill on a tile or vinyl floor is wiped up at once and dried with a dehumidifier and fans. Four things have to be true: clean water, a small area, immediate cleanup, and porous materials that were not significantly affected.

Situations That Need a Professional

Most of the water damage people actually face is beyond a do-it-yourself fix. Pick up the phone immediately when more than 10 square feet got wet, the water sat longer than a few hours, carpet, drywall, or another porous material was soaked, gray or black water is involved, moisture could be inside walls or the structure, the leak cannot be found or shut off, or a musty smell hints that mold has started.

The Hidden Costs of DIY Water Damage Cleanup

Many Redmond homeowners who attempt DIY water damage cleanup end up spending more than they would have with professional restoration. Here is why: incomplete drying leads to mold growth within days, adding $1,500 to $6,000 or more in remediation costs. Wet materials that are dried incorrectly keep breaking down, so more has to be replaced. The cost of rented extraction and drying gear, fans and dehumidifiers included, mounts quickly while performing below a professional setup. Improper cleanup documentation may reduce your insurance payout. Meanwhile the damage keeps compounding, since proper restoration cannot start until the unsuccessful DIY effort ends.

Why Professional Restoration Works Better

Professional water damage restoration provides several advantages that DIY cannot match: truck-mounted extraction that removes water hundreds of times faster, commercial drying equipment that actually dries wall cavities and structural materials, thermal imaging that finds hidden moisture, monitoring that verifies complete drying, antimicrobial treatment that helps prevent mold, thorough documentation that supports your insurance claim, and the peace of mind of knowing the job is done right.
