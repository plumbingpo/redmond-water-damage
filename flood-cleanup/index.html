Floods start in many ways: heavy rain, storm surge, a river over its banks, a dead sump pump, a sewer backup or a major plumbing failure, and all of them call for fast professional help. Expect gray water (Category 2) or black water (Category 3) in almost any flood, and with it bacteria, contamination and potentially hazardous substances that threaten your health. Day or night, 24/7, Redmond Restoration sends flood cleanup crews throughout Redmond to remove floodwater, sanitize the affected rooms and safely restore your home or building.

The first job is getting the water out quickly, and our submersible and truck-mounted pumps are able to clear thousands of gallons every hour. After that, IICRC-certified technicians on our team measure how contaminated the area is, then sanitize it with antimicrobial products, HEPA-filtered air cleaning and targeted disinfection of all surfaces the water reached. Anything unsalvageable comes out and is hauled off correctly, including wet insulation, drywall that took in contaminated water and waterlogged carpet padding.

After extraction and sanitizing, we set up commercial drying equipment, including LGR (low-grain refrigerant) dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers, to dry the structure of your property thoroughly. Daily readings from calibrated moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera tell us when everything is fully dry, which is what keeps mold from growing. Every stage of the job is recorded for your claim: photographs, detailed moisture maps, logs of the equipment used and the scope-of-work reports that insurance adjusters require.

How to Prevent Flood Cleanup Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Being outside a designated flood zone does not rule out flooding, so think about flood coverage
  • Extend your downspouts to carry rain from the roof out past the foundation
  • Install a battery backup sump pump so you stay protected when storms knock out power
  • Use waterproof bins and raised shelving for anything valuable kept at ground level or below
  • Regrade low spots so soil around the whole foundation falls away from the walls
  • Cracks in the foundation should be sealed and the basement walls given a waterproof coating

The Benefits of Professional Flood Cleanup

Getting the standing water out is only one part of cleaning up after a flood. Chemicals, bacteria, sewage and other contamination ride in with the flood and create real health dangers. Protecting your family from E. coli, hepatitis and similar waterborne illness takes proper containment, correct use of protective equipment (PPE) and proven sanitizing methods, all things professional teams train for.

Health is not the only issue: when cleanup is done wrong, moisture stays hidden and heavy mold can follow in a matter of days. Professional technicians use moisture meters, thermal imaging and psychrometric calculations to make sure every affected area, including wall cavities, subfloors and framing, reaches an acceptable drying standard. Nothing else reliably heads off secondary damage, and that damage can run thousands more to remediate, so we dry by the numbers.

Signs You Need Flood Cleanup in Redmond

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Water flooding out of a dishwasher, water heater or washing machine
  • Dirty water rising out of floor drains or sewer lines into rooms you use
  • Heavy rainfall, a dead sump pump and water spreading across the basement
  • Tide marks on drywall or trim where the water peaked
  • Roof or wall openings left by a storm, with rain pouring through
  • Cracked foundation walls, low doorways or window wells letting water inside
  • Water pooled on any floor of the building, no matter where it came from
