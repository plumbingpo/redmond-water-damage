Dealing With Sump Pump Failure Cleanup in Redmond

The moment a basement most needs its sump pump is often the moment the pump gives out. Heavy rain raises the water table, the pit fills, and then the power goes out, the float sticks or the pump cannot keep up. The pit overflows and the basement floor starts to flood. Nothing stops it until some pump is working again.

Do not think of it as clean water. Having passed through the ground, it usually holds bacteria, silt and drain tile residue, which makes it Category 2 at best, or Category 3 once it has been standing or sewage has mixed in. For the job, this means soaked porous items such as drywall and carpet pad are usually discarded, and what stays gets washed and disinfected first, then dried.

Right Now

What to Do Right Now

  1. Stay Out of the WaterDo not step into a flooded basement while the power is on. Water may be touching outlets, the furnace or the pump's own cord.
  2. Turn Off the Basement CircuitsTurn off the basement circuits at the breaker panel, but only if you can reach it from a dry spot. A panel surrounded by water is a job for the power company.
  3. Check the Pump SafelyLook to see whether the pump came unplugged, its outlet tripped or the float is pinned against the pit wall. Reaching into the pit or the water is not safe.
  4. Assume the Water Is DirtyIf going down cannot be avoided, put on gloves and rubber boots, leave pets and children upstairs, and wash up after handling wet items.
  5. Save What Is Dry, Then CallCarry dry valuables upstairs, photograph the water level and the pump, then call us. Waiting for it to recede without help is a mistake.
How We Fix It

How We Handle Sump Pump Failure Cleanup

1

Get the Water Out

Because the pumps and extraction units are ours, the broken sump does not hold up the work. Standing water and silt are removed from the floor, the pit area and any low spots.

2

Take Out What Got Wet

Carpet, padding, insulation and wet drywall that the water contacted are pulled by a crew in protective gear, bagged and removed from the property so the concrete and studs are open.

3

Wash and Disinfect

The slab, wall framing and remaining contents are washed down and treated with an antimicrobial product. Drying comes afterward, because fans on a dirty basement would blow contaminants everywhere.

4

Dry and Confirm

Dehumidifiers and air movers run until moisture meter readings on the framing and concrete return to normal, typically 3 to 5 days. Make sure a functioning pump is installed again by the time drying wraps up.

The Usual Culprits

How It Usually Starts

  • A pump too small for the volume of water entering the pit.
  • Stuck or snagged float switches, which never tell the pump to start.
  • Losing electricity in a storm when the pump has no battery backup.
  • Years of on and off cycling finally burning out an aging motor.
  • Loss of power at the pump alone, from a loose cord or a tripped circuit.
If It Is Left

What the Water Damages

Wall Framing

The wood plate at the base of each wall sits right on the slab and soaks up water first. Once the drywall is off, the plates and studs are washed, disinfected and dried.

Boxes and Stored Items

Contaminated water is absorbed fast by fabrics, books, cardboard and the legs of furniture. Within days mold shows up on absorbent things that were in it, and they are difficult to disinfect.

Basement Mechanical Equipment

The furnace and water heater frequently sit on the same floor as the pit. Controls, wiring and burners can be destroyed by water, so every appliance needs an inspection before it runs again.

Finished Basement Walls

Dirty water climbs from the slab into insulation and drywall, far higher than the line you can see. Since it is contaminated water, we remove the wet bottom portion instead of drying it.

Coverage

What About Insurance?

This is one of the most misunderstood claims. Water backing up or overflowing from a sump is usually excluded under a standard homeowners policy, and a flood policy is a different product with rules of its own. Often the only coverage comes from an added endorsement for water backup and sump overflow, which has limits of its own. Check the declarations page for it, or call your agent to find out. We record the pump failure, the water level and the damage so your claim file is complete.
